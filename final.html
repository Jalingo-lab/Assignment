About Us

Iliyasu Global Investment Nig. Ltd. is a trusted furniture manufacturing and interior solutions company specializing in the design, production, and installation of premium quality furniture for homes, offices, hotels, schools, churches, and commercial buildings. We combine skilled craftsmanship, high-quality materials, and modern designs to create elegant, durable, and functional furniture tailored to our clients' needs. From luxury home furniture and executive office furnishings to kitchen cabinets, wardrobes, TV stands, wooden doors, and custom-made furniture, we are committed to delivering exceptional quality, innovative designs, and outstanding customer satisfaction. Our dedication to excellence, professionalism, and timely delivery has earned us a reputation as a reliable partner for premium furniture solutions.
