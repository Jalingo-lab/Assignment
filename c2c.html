About C2C

More Than a Sports Center. It's an Experience.

C2C Sport Center Jalingo is a vibrant destination designed to bring sports, fitness, recreation, entertainment and hospitality together under one roof.

Whether you are looking to stay fit, enjoy a football match, relax by the pool, organize an event, enjoy great food or spend quality time with family and friends, C2C Sport Center provides an exciting environment for everyone.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, exciting and welcoming environment where people can connect, stay active and have fun.

Our Vision

To become a leading sports, recreation and entertainment destination in Taraba State.
